Recent updates.

The last three things that shipped. The source is open, so every change is on GitHub with the reasoning attached.

  1. 22 August 2026

    A first launch that shows you around

    Five cards walk a new writer through the real window — the sidebar, the tabs, the page, the notes rail, the guide — and then hand over to F1 for the rest. Word exports name themselves after the page you are on rather than the whole library, in a field you can correct before it saves.

  2. 21 August 2026

    Word export, fixed in the browser

    Exporting a Word document from the browser build asked for something only the desktop has, and died. One path now, the one both have — and the test presses the button and opens the file instead of reading the dialog, so it cannot break quietly again. The notes switch moved into the page bar, beside bold and the headings.

  3. 21 August 2026

    Comments in the selection bar, and a rail you can put away

    The bar that comes to a selection now carries a comment, which marks the words instead of replacing them. The rail of notes hides and comes back with Ctrl Shift Space, a control at the top of the rail, or the button in the top bar — which says how many notes are waiting behind it.
